Meta Events & Advertising Privacy Notice

How HealPath uses Meta tracking tools to measure campaigns while strictly safeguarding your medical confidentiality.

🛡️
Strict Clinical Data Guarantee

We never transmit symptoms, diagnoses, medical records, or consultation notes to Meta. All clinical interactions are handled exclusively on isolated, encrypted, HIPAA-aligned healthcare systems.

1. What We Track

Through the Meta Pixel and Conversions API (CAPI), we record non-clinical, aggregated user interactions on our public pages:

  • Page Visits: Information about which general pages or service summaries you browse.
  • Button & Link Clicks: Interactions with public buttons like "Contact Us" or "Learn More".
  • Lead Inquiries: Basic form completion (e.g. general contact request or WhatsApp chat click).
  • Technical Parameters: Browser type, anonymized IP, device info, and referrer URLs collected by Meta.

2. Why We Use This Information

  • To measure the effectiveness and reach of our healthcare awareness and educational campaigns.
  • To deliver relevant preventative health content to individuals who may benefit from it.
  • To optimize user journey performance and fix broken navigation or form flows.

3. Your Rights & How to Opt Out

You can adjust or opt out of Meta event tracking at any time:

  • Meta Ad Preferences: Manage off-Facebook activity tracking through your Meta Ad Settings.
  • Browser Controls: Block third-party cookies or utilize ad/script blockers in your browser.
  • Direct Request: Email us to request removal of your contact details from marketing lists.
