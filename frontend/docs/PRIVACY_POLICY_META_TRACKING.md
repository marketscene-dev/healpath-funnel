## Privacy Notice: Meta Events & Advertising Tracking

At **HealPath**, we believe healthcare relationships require complete trust, transparency, and data protection. This notice explains how we use Meta (Facebook & Instagram) tracking technologies on our public website and marketing funnels.

---

### 1. What We Track
When you browse our public website or landing pages, we use Meta Pixel and Meta Conversions API (CAPI) to record general website interactions, including:
* **Page Visits**: Which pages or care program descriptions you view.
* **Button Clicks**: Clicks on general links (such as "Learn More" or "Contact Us").
* **Lead / Form Completions**: General inquiry submissions, appointment request triggers, or WhatsApp chat initiation.
* **Technical Identifiers**: Anonymized IP addresses, browser types, and device identifiers collected by Meta.

---

### 2. What We NEVER Track or Share
> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Strict Clinical Data Isolation**: Your confidential medical data is never shared with Meta.

* **NO Health Records**: We never share medical history, diagnoses, symptoms, prescriptions, or clinical notes with Meta.
* **NO Consultation Details**: Doctor visits, tele-triage interactions, and test results are strictly confidential and hosted on secure, HIPAA-aligned, encrypted medical platforms completely isolated from marketing pixels.
* **NO Payment Credentials**: Payment and banking details are processed through encrypted, certified payment gateways and are never accessible to Meta.

---

### 3. Why We Use Meta Tracking
We utilize Meta advertising tools to:
* **Measure Campaign Performance**: Understand which educational healthcare content and care programs are helpful to our audience.
* **Deliver Relevant Information**: Show informative primary care updates and student/corporate health solutions to individuals who may benefit from them.
* **Optimize User Experience**: Identify navigation issues and improve the efficiency of our booking and inquiry funnels.

---

### 4. Legal Basis & Healthcare Compliance
Our data processing adheres to:
* **Meta Business Tools Terms** & Healthcare Advertising Policies.
* **Egyptian Personal Data Protection Law (Law No. 151 of 2020)**.
* **Global Privacy Standards & HIPAA-aligned** technical separation between marketing analytics and electronic health records (EHR).

---

### 5. How to Control or Opt Out
You have full control over how your data is used for advertising:
* **Meta Account Settings**: You can manage your ad preferences or turn off off-Facebook activity tracking anytime via [Meta Ad Preferences](https://www.facebook.com/adpreferences).
* **Browser Controls**: You can disable third-party cookies or install ad/tracker blockers in your browser settings.
* **Direct Opt-Out**: To request deletion of your contact records from our marketing lists, email us at [customercare@healpath.care](mailto:customercare@healpath.care).
